Creative Director, Brand & Film

Meridian Studio · Brooklyn, NY · Full-time

About Meridian
Meridian is an independent brand and film studio. We build identities and tell stories for consumer brands, cultural institutions and a handful of ambitious startups, from naming and visual identity through campaign films and experiential launches.

The role
We are looking for a Creative Director to lead a small, senior team across brand identity, campaign and film work. You will set the creative vision for client engagements, own the relationship with founders and marketing leaders, and direct external partners — directors, production companies, photographers, illustrators — so that every piece of work carries one idea all the way through.

What you'll do
- Lead concept development and creative direction for brand identity systems, campaigns and short-form film.
- Own client relationships with founders, CMOs and brand leads; present and defend the work; translate a client's intent into a brief the studio can execute.
- Select and direct directors, production partners and freelancers; hold the creative intent through production and post.
- Shape narrative: decide whose story a piece tells and what it is really about.
- Bring emerging technology into the work where it serves the idea — interactive installations, generative tools, AI in the process.
- Mentor designers and art directors; run critique; raise the craft bar across the studio.
- Contribute to new business: pitches, proposals, and the studio's own initiatives.

Who you are
- 10+ years in brand and campaign work, with at least 3 leading creative for an agency, studio or in-house team.
- A portfolio of identity systems and films where you can explain what you decided and why, and what the director, the crew and the team contributed.
- Comfortable in the room with executives; comfortable on set with a crew.
- Experience working across markets and cultures; Japanese-market experience is a strong plus.
- Curiosity about new technology as a creative material, not as a feature.

Nice to have
- Experience with experiential or interactive work: installations, events, live formats.
- Hands-on with generative tools or creative coding.

Benefits
Health, dental, vision. Four weeks of paid vacation. Studio closed the last week of December.
