Senior Product Designer, Payments

Stripe · San Francisco, New York, or Remote (US)

About Stripe
Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies, from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups, use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

About the team
The Payments Design team shapes how businesses of every size accept money: Checkout, Payment Links, the Payment Element, and the merchant-facing surfaces in the Stripe Dashboard where operators configure, monitor and troubleshoot their payment flows. We partner closely with product management, engineering, data science and go-to-market teams across the company, and we ship to users in 45+ countries.

What you'll do
- Own the end-to-end design of new and existing payments surfaces, from early problem framing and 0 to 1 exploration through high-fidelity prototypes and shipped product.
- Partner with product managers, engineers and data scientists to define product strategy, prioritize a roadmap, and make trade-offs explicit.
- Design for developers and operators alike: API-first flows, Dashboard workflows, and the checkout experiences their customers see.
- Contribute to and extend Sail, Stripe's design system, so that patterns hold up across dozens of product teams and remain accessible.
- Use AI thoughtfully: explore where models and agents can remove work from merchants, and prototype those experiences in code where it helps the team decide.
- Run and synthesize user research with merchants, and use product analytics and A/B testing to inform decisions.
- Present work to senior leadership and cross-functional stakeholders; raise the quality bar through critique and mentorship.
- Work across a complex, regulated, global organization where compliance and risk are part of the brief, not an afterthought.

Who you are
We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements
- 5+ years of product design experience shipping software used by businesses and consumers.
- A portfolio that demonstrates strong interaction design, systems thinking and visual craft.
- Experience contributing to or maintaining a design system at scale.
- Fluency in Figma and comfort prototyping in code (HTML/CSS/JavaScript or React).
- A track record of partnering with engineering and product to ship, including navigating ambiguity and undefined problem spaces.
- Excellent written and verbal communication; you can explain the why behind a decision to executives and to engineers.

Preferred qualifications
- Experience with payments, fintech, financial services or other regulated domains.
- Experience designing AI-powered features, conversational or agentic experiences, or human-in-the-loop workflows.
- Experience leading design for a 0→1 product or new business line inside a large organization.
- Experience working with international teams and localization; Japanese language ability is a plus for our APAC merchants.

Hybrid work at Stripe
Office-assigned Stripes spend at least 50% of the time in a given month in their local office or with users. This hits a balance between bringing people together for in-person collaboration and learning from each other, while supporting flexibility about how to do this in a way that makes sense for individuals and their teams.
