The Opportunity

A 150-year-old family barbershop, underground and invisible from the street — 90% male clientele, zero repeat customers. The new owner's vision: transform it into a modern salon that women and younger clients would choose.

My Direction

I started with the business, not the aesthetics: an innovation bootcamp with the CEO, journey mapping, and a business-model redesign built around ongoing customer relationships. The strategic anchor became the tagline "Kitadoko as a group of personal hair stylists" — a salon positioned not as a place, but as people. The identity, letterforms, service flow, and renovated interiors all followed from that one idea.
