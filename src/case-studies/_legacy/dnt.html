The Opportunity

The Tokyo Metropolitan Government set an ambitious target: virtually zero CO2 emissions by 2050. To achieve this, they needed more than policy — they needed a platform that could engage citizens, corporations, and especially youth in sustainability. The challenge was to make climate action feel accessible, creative, and participatory rather than bureaucratic.

My Direction

DO! NUTS TOKYO was launched as a sub-brand from the Value Frontier rebranding — applying the brand DNA developed in that project to a citizen-facing sustainability platform. I led comprehensive branding, digital platform design, and workshop facilitation. The brand identity featured a vibrant, approachable visual system with multiple logo configurations for different contexts.

The digital platform served as the central hub for content, events, and community engagement. I designed workshop frameworks that generated innovative sustainability proposals from youth ambassadors — expanding the program to its third cohort. These proposals influenced Japanese businesses and demonstrated that design-led engagement could drive meaningful environmental action.
