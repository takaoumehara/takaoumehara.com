The Problem Space 問題空間

Shopping on a TV should feel effortless from the couch. But three long-standing problems block that promise: remote text entry is painful (navigating an on-screen keyboard with arrow keys feels like archaeology), browsing is slow (navigating thousands of products with a D-pad creates fatigue), and voice alone can't show enough context (asking for "running shoes" without seeing options leaves you guessing). The experience gets stuck between "lean-back" convenience and "lean-in" detail — no single input mode solves the whole journey. テレビでの買い物は快適であるべきだが、3 つの課題がその約束を阻む。リモコンでの文字入力は苦痛で、D-pad で何千もの商品を閲覧するのは遅く、音声だけでは十分な文脈を示せない。「リラックス」と「詳細確認」の間で体験が行き詰まる。

The Approach アプローチ

I designed a 10-foot commerce interface where the TV stays the stage, and the phone becomes the best possible input device — combining the strengths of all three modes without their compromises. D-pad navigation handles casual discovery across product rails. Alexa-style voice handles direct intent ("show me chocolate protein powder," "add this to my cart," "checkout"). And when you need a keyboard for search, a precise pointer for product detail, or a private mic for voice, your phone is already paired via a single QR scan — no app download, no login screen, just instant control. The pairing layer is built on my own open-source snap-pair pattern, which handles room codes, presence heartbeat, and automatic cleanup. テレビはステージとして残し、スマホを最適な入力デバイスにした。3 つのモードの長所を妥協なく組み合わせた。D-pad で商品レールをカジュアルに閲覧。Alexa 音声で直接の意図を伝える（「チョコレートプロテインを見せて」「カートに追加」「チェックアウト」）。検索キーボード、商品詳細ポインター、プライベートマイクが必要な時は QR スキャンで接続済み。ダウンロードもログインも不要。接続層は自作のオープンソース snap-pair パターンで実装。

What the prototype does

TV shopping, redesigned for 10 feet
Home rails, product pages, filters, cart, and checkout — all laid out for distant viewing with oversized focus rings and high-contrast dark cinema UI.
Alexa for Shopping voice flows
Press V or the mic button and say things like “show me chocolate protein,” “add this to my cart,” or “checkout.” A local intent parser routes the command.
Phone becomes remote, keyboard, mic
Scan the QR code on the TV and the phone becomes a D-pad, joystick, trackpad, text search box, and push-to-talk Alexa mic — instantly.
snap-pair underneath
The pairing layer is my open-source snap-pair pattern: short code as a rendezvous handle, server-validated room membership, presence heartbeat, and automatic cleanup.
Adaptive cross-device state
Cart count, current screen, and search context are mirrored to the phone in real time, so the remote always knows what the TV is showing.
Zero build tooling
Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No bundler, no npm, no framework lock-in. It runs from a folder or any static host.
