Challenge 課題

Facial exercise and rehab tools feel clinical; games that use the camera usually feel creepy or finicky. We needed play that reads as joy in thirty seconds, keeps video on-device, and teaches itself without a tutorial wall. 顔の運動やリハビリは臨床っぽく、カメラを使うゲームは気味悪さや反応の悪さが先に立つ。30秒で喜びとして読め、映像は端末内に留め、説明書なしで伝わる遊びが必要だった。

Solution 解決策

Kao Game maps mouth, cheeks, and gaze into mini-games that reward clear motion, not precision suffering. Detection stays local; the decisive move was designing rules players can infer from what moves on screen — then cutting anything that needed a sentence. 口・頬・視線を、はっきり動いたときに報いるミニゲームへ写す。検出は端末内。決定打は、画面の動きからルールを推測できる設計にし、一文が必要なものを削ること。
