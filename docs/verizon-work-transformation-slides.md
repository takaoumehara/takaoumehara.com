## 5. Empathy applied to the internal team
- Innovation starts with empathy—not only for customers, but for the team.
- In **late 2025**, the presenter conducted deep-dive interviews with **16 designers** to map their reality beyond deliverables: creative process, daily hurdles, and friction points limiting velocity.
- Insights identified where operational drag was stifling creativity. The response is a new toolkit using AI to address struggles voiced by the team.
- Intended shift: designers spend less time on process and more time on design.

## 6. Quantifying the Friction: The “Heat Map” of Our Design Process
- **Method:** “We didn’t just collect feedback; we measured it.”
- The **16 interview transcripts** were categorized into **over 40 distinct friction points**, ranked by **Severity, Business Impact, and Ease of Change**.
- **Table/diagram in words:** A heat-map matrix lists ID, category, friction-point symptom, and 1–10 rating columns (including Severity, Business Impact, Ease of Change, and AI Opportunity).

## 7. Designers’ Frictions
Four recurring problems are made explicit:

1. **Tribal Knowledge Trap** — Critical business logic, project requirements, and institutional knowledge are fragmented across email and “tribal knowledge,” not a centralized repository. This creates costly inconsistencies/rework and risks loss of product intelligence when staff leave.
2. **Late-Phase Discovery and rework** — Delayed technical discovery and lack of early SME collaboration leave edge cases to be identified post-handoff, forcing emergency rework and disrupting design.
3. **Designing in the Dark** — Without dedicated budget or timeline for user testing, designers rely on subjective/unfounded stakeholder opinions instead of validated data; there is no objective way to justify decisions or prove effectiveness.
4. **Strategic Design Compression** — Pressure for rapid delivery pushes designers into “pixel pushing,” sacrificing the deep psychological and cognitive rigor needed to act as strategic partners rather than production resources.

## 12. The “Project Master Brain”
- **Definition:** A single, centralized, constantly learning knowledge hub that serves as the project’s ultimate source of truth.
- **Three intended benefits:**
  - **Synthesized Answers:** Even when information is massive for a project, knowledge can be synthesized in whatever form is needed.
  - **Eliminates Tribal Knowledge:** Less reliance on a few individuals with deep brand/project knowledge.
  - **Continuous Evolving Brain:** New information is uploaded as the project continues so the hub stays current.
- **Primary implementation shown:** NotebookLM / Project Master Brain.

## 13. Project Master Brain inputs
- **Center diagram:** NotebookLM “Project Master Brain,” with arrows from four input domains:
  - **Project Documentation:** Scope, business documents, CX strategy documents, and technical requirements.
  - **Organizational Knowledge:** Recorded and transcribed Webex meeting transcripts, capturing context and rationale.
  - **Strategic Direction:** Explicit feedback and knowledge from Associate Directors and Executive Leaders.
  - **Customer & Market Intelligence:** Industry trends, competitor websites, and YouTube transcripts of service comparisons.
- **Implication:** The hub combines formal artifacts, tacit organizational context, leadership direction, and external market evidence.

## 14. Utilizing Multiple Master Brains

- This suggests a composable architecture: reusable brand/research knowledge bases are combined for project-specific synthesis.

## Reusable facts / strongest portfolio signals
- Research-backed problem framing: **16 designer interviews** and **40+ friction points** converted qualitative pain into a ranked heat map.
- The core intervention is not “AI everywhere”; it is a staged workflow that inserts alignment, early edge-case detection, functional prototyping, and synthetic usability testing before handoff.
- Four named agents/solutions map directly to four designer failure modes: **Master Brain**, **Edge Case Detector AI Agent**, **Principal CX Strategist AI Agent**, and **Strategic Design AI Agents WDS**.
- The Master Brain is positioned as a continuously updated, centralized source of truth that synthesizes project, organizational, strategic, and market knowledge.
- The deck’s strongest outcome claims are qualitative—less process/rework, earlier discovery, more evidence-based decisions, and more strategic designer time. No measured time-saved, adoption, quality, revenue, or usability-lift KPI is shown in these 15 slides.
