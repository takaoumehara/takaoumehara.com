Crowd drawing game

Scan the code on the wall, scribble with a finger, flick it — and your doodle lands on the big screen as a creature that swims, meets a stranger's drawing, and breeds with it.

A full room is the reward, not the failure mode.

Two doodles that meet produce a child whose outline is a real blend of both parents. The wall gets richer with every person who joins.

Where it belongs

  • A launch, a party, a festival wall The moment the audience stops watching and starts putting things on the screen.
  • A lobby or a booth with a queue Nobody is briefed and nobody is handed anything — the QR code on the wall is the whole invitation.
  • A classroom or a workshop Thirty drawings arrive at once and the room can see what it made together.
