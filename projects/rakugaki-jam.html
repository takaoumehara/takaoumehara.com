Rakugaki Jam: Doodle on mobile, fling to the wall

Draw freely on your phone, then fling your stroke onto a shared projection screen with a flick of your finger. A live, crowd-drawn collaborative visual jam powered by Snap Pair and Firebase Realtime Database.

01

Personal intimacy meets public spectacle.

Drawing on a large stage in front of people induces performance anxiety. Drawing privately in the palm of your hand and flicking it up transforms self-conscious hesitation into a joyous, playful toss.

02

Powered by the Snap Pair protocol.

Built on our open-source Snap Pair architecture. The wall acts as the room host; audience members connect via ephemeral room tokens, automatically receiving designated color palettes to maintain visual harmony.

03

Organic kinetic canvas dynamics.

Rather than accumulating clutter like a static whiteboard, thrown doodles gently drift, react to ambient audio amplitude, and slowly dissolve over time to preserve momentum and freshness throughout an event.

Event & physical installations

  • Conferences and keynote venues: Audience questions or visual reactions submitted as expressive sketches rather than dry text polls.
  • Live music, club nights & VJ sets: Crowd participation where the audience visually co-creates the visual backdrop behind the performers.
