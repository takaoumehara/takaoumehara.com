01 · The model broke

The old fundraiser could not simply return.

Through 2018 and 2019, the festival generated roughly $10,000 or more in annual revenue largely through food. Parent volunteers working in food businesses supplied donated or heavily discounted products. After the COVID pause, restrictions, lost suppliers, and changed school operations meant the 2022 festival could not rely on that structure at all.
