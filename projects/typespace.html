Keeping nothing is the feature.

When a text box is a record you cannot write badly, so nobody writes. Nothing here survives the minute you wrote it in, and that is the only reason people type.

Where it belongs

  • A reception, an anniversary, a memorial Every guest sends one word from their own phone and watches it join the others.
  • The close of a conference A room of people answering one question at once, on the screen they are already facing.
  • A quiet screen in a lobby or a shop It plays itself when nobody is typing, and anyone passing can write into it.
Input
Typing — a keyboard, or a paired phone across the room
Output
A sky on any screen, from a laptop to a projection wall
