Hand-tracking music instrument

Pinch the air and a string is strung there that rings. Seven browser works, each built on exactly one rule between something your body does and something you see and hear.

The beauty came from refusing the longer list.

The usual answer to "make it more beautiful" is more libraries. The wager here was the opposite: one physical model driving both the sound and the picture.

Where it belongs

  • A stage, with a dancer or a crowd in front of it One camera and a wall; what the body does is what the room hears.
  • A permanent piece in a lobby or a gallery It plays itself when the room is empty and answers the first person who walks up.
  • A delivered build with a client's colours and sound in it One URL, no server to run afterwards, and nothing to maintain.
Input
Hands or face, through an ordinary camera. No microphone, no controller
Output
Sound and light on one screen, or on a projection wall
Runs on
TypeScript · MediaPipe on the device · a hand-written AudioWorklet · WebGL2

2026, by our founder, with AI coding agents
