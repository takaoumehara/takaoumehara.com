Mobile arcade shooting game

Drawn entirely in emoji. One phone shows a QR code, up to four scan it, and they are all in the same game — on a stage that reads the weather where you are playing.

You join by pointing a camera at a code.

One phone opens a room and puts a QR code on the screen; the others scan it, or type the six characters under it. No app, no account, no install — and no console in the room.

Where it belongs

  • Four people in a room with their own phones One screen shows the code, the rest scan it, and the room is the console.
  • A branded game that ships as a folder A brand's characters in place of the emoji, delivered as static files any host can serve.
  • A daily reason to come back A stage a whole market plays on the same day, and a score card worth sending to somebody.
Input
Up to four phones, a finger dragging on each; a keyboard on a desktop
Output
The same world on every screen; lives and the boss's health are shared
Runs on
Plain JavaScript modules · Canvas · Web Audio · WebRTC data channels
