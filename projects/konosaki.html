The Brand

Konosaki is a bilingual consulting partner connecting Japanese and global businesses. The identity is built on a single idea — a bridge: a clear, confident path across languages, cultures, and markets. I designed and built the complete brand system — from strategy and logo to guidelines, website, and collateral — AI-assisted, through Creativity Is Everywhere LLC.

The Logo

The primary lockup places the symbol at the heart of the name — the bridge sits between kono ("this") and saki ("ahead"), literally connecting the present to what comes next. The symbol always leans forward, suggesting motion across borders.

The Website

The identity lives at konosaki-co.vercel.app. A bilingual site with parallax storytelling and seamless page transitions — designed and built to demonstrate the brand system in motion. Every interaction reinforces the "bridge" concept: smooth crossings between Japanese and English, between sections, between ideas.
