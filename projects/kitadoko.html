Branded Experience / Est. 1871, Tokyo

Kitadoko

A 150-year-old barbershop, underground and invisible from the street, with zero repeat customers. Rebuilt end to end — brand, business model, service, and the room itself.

Client
Kitadoko Hair Salon
Role
Creative Director & Design Strategist
Heritage
Established 1871 — 150+ years
Categories
Rebranding · Service Design · Space Design
01 / Opportunity

A family barbershop below street level, with 90% male clientele and no returning customers. The new owner's brief was not a logo. It was a question about who the shop was for.

02 / Direction

I started with the business, not the aesthetics: an innovation bootcamp with the CEO, journey mapping, and a business-model redesign built around ongoing customer relationships.

The strategic anchor became a single line — Kitadoko as a group of personal hair stylists — a salon positioned not as a place but as people. The identity, the letterforms, the service flow and the renovated interior all followed from that one idea.

03 / Scope
  • Brand DNA & strategy
  • Business model design
  • Service design
  • Brand identity
  • Space & interior design
  • Logo development
04 / Results

Measured against the shop as inherited.

90% Repeat customer rate — from 0%
91% Satisfaction, 4–5★ — from 62%
200 Monthly visits per stylist — from 30
50% Female clientele — from 10%
