Ten-foot shopping interface

A sofa-distance commerce prototype with three inputs: D-pad navigation, Alexa-style voice, and a phone that pairs in one scan to become a remote, keyboard, pointer, and microphone.

Self-initiated concept. Not affiliated with, or commissioned by, Amazon.

Distance changes the interface.

Large type, visible focus, short rows, and predictable D-pad movement replace the dense controls that work at arm’s length.

Where it belongs

  • A living-room commerce prototypeTest product discovery and repeat purchase at sofa distance.
  • A multi-device interaction workshopExplore when the television should lead and when a phone should take over.
  • A ten-foot design-system studyEvaluate focus, legibility, voice fallback, and remote constraints before production.
