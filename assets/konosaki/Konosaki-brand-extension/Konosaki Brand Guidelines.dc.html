01

The Brand

ブランドについて

Konosaki is a bilingual consulting partner connecting Japanese and global businesses. The identity is built on a single idea — a bridge: a clear, confident path across languages, cultures, and markets.

Trusted

Calm, precise, and professional — a partner, not a vendor.

Modern

Clean geometry and generous space over ornament.

Forward

The mark always leans ahead — motion across borders.

02

Logo

ロゴ

The primary lockup places the symbol at the heart of the name — the bridge sits between kono (“this”) and saki (“ahead”), literally connecting the present to what comes next.

Symbol
Vertical lockup
Wordmark
Clear space

Keep space equal to the symbol’s height clear on all sides of the lockup.

Minimum size

Lockup: 120 px / 30 mm wide. Symbol alone: 24 px / 8 mm.

Backgrounds

Use only on white, Paper, black, or Deep Harbor. Never recolor the symbol.
