## Design Context

### Users

Hiring managers, design leaders, engineering partners, and prospective clients reviewing Takao Umehara as a senior product designer and hands-on builder. Their job is to understand his range quickly, open credible work, and decide whether to start a conversation.

### Brand Personality

Modern, assured, and technically fluent. The interface should feel calm but decisive: design-led without fragility, engineering-aware without developer cosplay, and polished without decoration for its own sake.

### Aesthetic Direction

Structured Tech Editorial: a warm light ground, firm sans-serif hierarchy, mono used only for metadata, strict rectangular grids, near-black type, and one high-contrast blue accent. The signature interaction is spatial continuity from a selected project rectangle into its full-screen reading view and back. Avoid serif display type, glass, glow, generic gradients, excessive rounding, masonry, bounce, or persistent ambient motion.

### Design Principles

1. Make proof scannable: category, title, role, and status should resolve before decorative detail.
2. Use strict alignment and decisive type weight to communicate both systems thinking and visual craft.
3. Reserve motion for spatial explanation and immediate feedback; opening and closing must feel like the same object changing state.
4. Keep interaction fast: visible response under 100ms, transition under 500ms, transform and opacity only.
5. Treat accessibility as part of the visual system: WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and reflow, visible focus, keyboard parity, and a no-spatial-motion reduced-motion path.
