About New York / Creativity Is Everywhere LLC

I started in Tokyo and have spent the last twenty years in New York.

At Amplify I designed learning experiences used by millions of children; at T-Mobile a buy-online-pick-up-in-store system spanning kiosk, staff app and voice; at Verizon the post-login experience for a prepaid platform with millions of subscribers. Alongside that, a 150-year-old Tokyo barbershop and visual directions for Coca-Cola.

Around 2023 the centre of gravity moved to AI, for a simple reason: that is where the unsolved problems are. But I did not start there. I know what was hard before agents existed — which is the only way to tell whether an agent has actually made something better, or just made it faster to produce something worse.
