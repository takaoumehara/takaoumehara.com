About

Strategy in the room. Craft in the file.

Takao Umehara is a product designer, innovation strategist, and creative technologist based in New York City. His work combines strategic vision with hands-on design, rapid prototyping, and production-grade engineering.

He started in Tokyo and has spent the last twenty years in New York, designing products and systems for organizations including Amplify, T-Mobile, Verizon, Coca-Cola, and XQ.
